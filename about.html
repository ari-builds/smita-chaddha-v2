About my practice

My first priority is building a comfortable, respectful working relationship — one that lets you begin to focus on the difficult, painful, or complex issues that brought you to counseling. I listen carefully and help guide discussions that support real change: resolving conflict, reducing the weight of anxiety and depressed mood, and learning to manage anger and overwhelm.

I am a trauma and EMDR specialist and mindfulness-based therapist. My focus is helping clients shift unproductive thinking patterns and coping strategies, drawing on current research in neuroscience, positive neuroplasticity, DBT, and mindfulness-based therapy. This work supports clients navigating Complex PTSD, grief and loss, and comorbid depression and anxiety, as well as high anxiety, phobia, and impulsivity — with the goal of stronger emotional regulation, distress tolerance, and communication.

The two approaches people ask about most are EMDR and Internal Family Systems (IFS). I also weave in somatic (Sensorimotor) techniques, because trauma lives in the body as much as it lives in the story.

I love diversity and am genuinely interested in getting to know your unique personality and culture. I am compassionate and engaging in session, and I work to create a safe environment built on respect and non-judgment.

My goal is to increase mindfulness in thought and action — building emotional regulation, distress tolerance, and better communication.
